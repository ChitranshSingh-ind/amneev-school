## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment**: While the typography pairing (*Cormorant Garamond* + *Plus Jakarta Sans*) and warm ivory/forest palette provide a solid foundational tone, the **macro-layout and component structures suffer from severe "AI-generated SaaS template" syndrome**. The site over-relies on repetitive 2-column splits with checkmark grids, excessive card containers, formulaic centered headlines, and rigid symmetrical 3-card/4-card rows.

**Deterministic Scan**: 0 mechanical CSS syntax bugs, but structural analysis reveals 19 consecutive sections with identical vertical padding (`py-20 sm:py-28`), 8 sections using identical 2-column layout templates, and checkmark icons repeated across 7 distinct sections.
