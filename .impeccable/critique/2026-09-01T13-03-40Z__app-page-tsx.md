## Design Specificity Verdict

**Verdict:** **High-End Bespoke Institutional Portal**. The homepage has been completely distilled from a 19-section template into 6 cinematic, narrative chapters. All card container fatigue, SaaS checkmark checklists, pulsing status dots, and artificial dark vignettes have been eliminated in favor of magazine-grade editorial layouts, authentic CBSE leadership, and spacious typography.
