A flagship, world-class digital presence for AMNEEV Vision School that reflects its 15+ years of educational excellence, combining rigorous CBSE academics with modern innovation (Atal Tinkering Lab, technology collaborations), holistic character building (NCC, sports, arts), and an empowering, nurturing campus community.

## Positioning

## Operating Context

Evaluated by parents seeking high-caliber academic preparation and balanced character development; accessible across desktop and mobile devices; fast-loading with rich editorial photography, typography, and clear conversion pathways for 2026–27 admissions.

## Product Principles

1. **Editorial Sophistication over Template Noise:** Generous layout pacing, deliberate typography, and magazine-grade storytelling instead of repetitive card grids.
2. **Honesty and Academic Substance:** Celebrate real infrastructure, certified curriculum, and student empowerment without inflated claims or artificial gimmicks.
3. **Frictionless Discovery & Admissions:** Clear wayfinding, multi-tier program clarity (Early Years, Middle, Senior), transparent admission walkthrough, and accessible inquiry flows.
4. **Resilient Architecture & Performance:** Fast load times, responsive across all viewports (375px to 1920px+), keyboard accessibility, and centralized asset management.
